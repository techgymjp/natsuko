[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
You make the call at the center of history's crises.

The White House, thirteen days from nuclear war. Mission Control as Apollo 13 bleeds oxygen. The cockpit of an airliner that has just lost both engines.
The information is incomplete, time is short, and your AI advisors are sometimes convincingly wrong.
What do you trust, and what do you choose? Command Seat is a history simulation that trains your judgment like a muscle.

■ 20 MISSIONS
• The Cuban Missile Crisis, Apollo 13, the Battle of Midway, the Spanish Armada, Crossing the Delaware, Shackleton's Endurance, the Chilean mine rescue, the Miracle on the Hudson and more
• Start with the easiest mission. Finish one and the next one unlocks
• Five decisions per mission, each against the clock. Running out of time counts as deciding not to decide

■ HOW IT TRAINS YOUR JUDGMENT
• Some incoming reports are false. Verify them, but verifying costs time
• Three AI advisors weigh in: cautious, aggressive and data-driven. One of them may be wrong for reasons that sound right
• State your confidence with every decision and learn whether you are over- or underconfident
• Results involve luck. Sound calls can fail and poor calls can get lucky

■ AFTER EVERY MISSION
• See your outcome and what really happened in history
• A matrix that rates your judgment separately from your result
• Five axes: judgment, speed, info checks, AI scrutiny and calibration
• Each false report revealed, and the hole in each advisor's reasoning
• A what-if timeline that branches wherever you chose differently from history
• History did not always choose best, and the review explains why

■ KEEP TRAINING
• Today's decision: one historical dilemma a day, done in 3 minutes (30 questions). Build your streak
• Judgment profile: your decision habits, drawn from all your missions
• Compare with a friend: swap codes and see your decisions side by side

■ ALSO
• Music and sound change with each mission: Morse code, spacecraft radio tones, cracking ice and more
• English and Japanese
• No account needed. Your records stay on your device
• The history is based on widely known sources but simplified for the game

Can your judgment change history?
